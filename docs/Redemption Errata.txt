Combat

P183: Plasma Weapon Attacks should be +2 Multiplier versus shields, not "Double Damage" as described here. This brings it in line with the description for Counter: Shields on P212.
P183: Terran Sphere psionics using force fields circumvent the restriction on Psionic effects by having a gap in their force field's protection. This means that their force fields may be bypassed by aimed or area attacks that happen while they are using their powers. Normally this vulnerability only lasts for the Psionics turn, leaving them vulnerable to sustained attacks, but ongoing effects require the gap to remain open and exploitable.
P185: Armor Reduction from Coordinated Fire or from attacks with Counter: Armor persists until the end of the round, allowing subsequent players to take advantage of the target's weakened state.
P191: Battle strain can be spent on a 1 to 1 basis to increase the number of missiles in a salvo. This can not be used in conjunction with the Coordinated fire effect.
P191: Using Battle Strain to Activate Tags or Perform Extra Actions with a unit does not fall under the single Battle Strain expenditure per round rule. Additional extra actions after the first cost increasing multiples of 3 Battle Strain (6, 9, etc...) as per the extra actions for AP rule.
P191: A character attached to a unit may use Tags and their personal AP to skill switch from the unit's target number on to their corresponding skill.
P194: Missile attacks reduce their targets' total margin of defense by the number of attacking missiles in the salvo not -1 per salvo launched at the target. This reduction is not restricted to their bonus margin of defense from anti missile fire.
Item Creation

P209-212: Software no longer requires a computer rank by size. Instead, program size sets the computer grade it needs. Tiny, Small, and Medium programs need a Minor computer, carry 1 or 2 Tasks, and serve a single user. Large and Huge programs need a Moderate computer, carry multiple Tasks, and serve dozens of users at once. Colossal programs need a Major computer and serve hundreds of users at once. Computer rank no longer limits program size; it only caps Task target numbers. A Task runs at its own TN or the computer rank's maximum TN, whichever is lower. Replace every "Required Computer Rank" in program stat blocks with the matching Required Computer Grade.
P210: Omission of Turret rules from Attack attribute - Spending the base cost for an attack multiple times allows additional gunners to make attacks with the weapon, or the same gunner assuming he spends the action points. The Ships of the Line design exercise starting on page 219 provides an example of these rules in action.
P210: Melee attacks can never gain the Area attribute.
P210: Rail (Kinetic Ranged) weapons integrated into an item (ship and vehicle weapons, and units slaved to a host such as suit-mounted or Weapon Link weapons) draw a Power Slot like any other Attack, in addition to their Ammunition. Standalone rail weapons (hand weapons such as gauss pistols and rifles) need no Power Supply: the power is built into the casing of each round, and this is already included in their Ammunition.
P210: The Hyperspace implementation (the Shohan Dreadnought's hyper cannon, the only such attack in the game) includes Counter: Armor and Area: Small Sudden at no extra cost. Both are printed in the cannon's stat block so its effects can be read without looking up the implementation.
P210: An Attack on a One-Time Use item, such as a grenade, needs no feed (no Power Slot and no Ammunition): the item is its own charge.
P211: A Plasma Attack's free Bleed (half its Weapon Multiplier, at the grade matching the Attack's scale) can be increased. Additional Bleed damage costs the difference between the full Bleed cost and the free Bleed.
P211: Plasma has a self-powered implementation for hand weapons. Instead of drawing 2 Power Slots per mount, it needs special ammunition (an Ammunition Resource). That requirement is built into the implementation as a Moderate limitation: it takes one of the two Moderate limitation slots but refunds no Build Points. Plasma weapons built into or slaved to a host still draw 2 Power Slots per mount, with or without ammunition. Kinetic (rail) weapons split the same way (p210): Kinetic draws a Power Slot plus Ammunition, Kinetic (self-powered) uses Ammunition only.
P212: Counter Armor no longer can bypass non-partial armor on an aimed attack. Instead on an unaimed attack it will reduce armor by 1. Aimed attacks will grant an additional 1 armor reduction for the attack, bringing the aimed attack to -2 armor. However, the armor reduction contributed to other attacks remains at -1 armor.
P212-215: Counter: Strain raises or lowers Psionic Strain by 5 per Counter (20 BP each). Regeneration: Strain restores Psionic Strain like other Regeneration implementations. A drug's Duration is bought as a Resource (1 Minor rank ≈ 10 combat rounds or 1 hour; 1 Moderate rank ≈ 1 day).
P213: Reactionless drives should not expel Reaction Mass.
P213: A Drive needs no Fuel Resource when it can run from its item's own Power Supply and that supply burns no Fuel: a Coil (battery), Environmental, or Hyperspace Tap supply. A creature's legs, wings, or fins are a Biological Drive, which needs no Fuel either. Light Sail and Jump drives are unchanged.
P214: Hangar: "multiple items" means 3 items, or 4 if crammed in. For example, a Major Hangar on a Huge ship holds 3 Large craft (4 crammed), and a Moderate Hangar holds 3 items two sizes smaller (4 crammed).
P214: Launchers are bought in sets of 4 (one set draws 1 Power Slot and costs 4 × the per-launcher price: 20 / 40 / 60 BP). Ships printing launcher counts that are not multiples of 4 are corrected by errata (Standard Assault Shuttle, Dragon, Conventional Frigate, Redemption, Havenite Marauder). A Magazine does not have to match the launcher count.
P215: Add a Link type, Refueling: a docking connection that transfers fuel to a linked craft of up to the Link's maximum size. Each Refueling Link draws 1 Power Slot of its grade while in use. (The Fleet Auxiliary's three Major Refueling Links account for 3 of its 4 used Power Slots.)
P216: A Moderate Power Supply may be installed on a Small item (the table lists Medium). Major Power Supplies still require a Large item, except that the compact Hyperspace Tap and Coil implementations can squeeze a Major Power Supply into a Medium item.
P216: An item with no Power Supply or Drive of its own, such as a weapon module or a G3P, draws the Power Slots it uses from the host it is mounted on or plugged into. Its stat block lists them as its power requirement. A Slave limitation still means it needs a Link of the right type to work, not just a power connection.
P217: A Fuel Resource can feed Drives and Power Supplies of its own grade or lower. A larger fuel tank can feed a smaller drive (for example, Major Fuel can feed a Moderate Reaction Drive), but never the reverse.
P217: Shohan Force Field Regeneration is always Major grade (regenerates every round).
P217-218: Key Personnel (the item needs a specific crew member, such as a Navigator) is a Moderate limitation. A Modifier covering several skills is costed per skill (10 BP × rank × number of skills). A Minor Reaction Drive is a jet pack that works on a planet or in microgravity.
P217-218: The grade of a Modifier is how many skills it covers: Minor for one skill (10 BP per rank), Moderate for two (20 BP per rank), and Major for three or more, or a whole class of skills such as "Weapons" or "Technical Skills" (30 BP per rank). This replaces costing multi-skill Modifiers per skill. The +4 limit per skill still applies; how a class Modifier stacks with others is left to the table.
