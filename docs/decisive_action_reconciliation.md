# Decisive Action: equipment reconciliation (first pass, 2026-10-05)

Source: *Decisive Action*, Chapter 6 (Field Kit, pp101–135); new attributes from Chapter 5 (Arms Race). Built with the same Workbench rules, rulings and errata as the core Armory and the Deployment Companion. **Nothing here is in the catalog or errata yet**; this is the review copy.

Gap column: `OK` = matches the target CR (printed CR, or printed − 1 for items sold per 10); `+N` = N BP short of the printed CR's band; `-N` = N BP over it. Shohan gear prints CR —, so it has no target.

## Rulings needed before this goes into the catalog

1. ~~**Regeneration and power.**~~ **Decided 2026-10-05: keep the ruling.** Force Field and Mechanical Regeneration each use their own Power Slot; Biological doesn't. Decisive Action's power lines leave Regeneration out, so these get errata when they enter the catalog:

   | Item | Printed | Corrected |
   |---|---|---|
   | Auxiliary Armor, Stage 1 and 2 | 3 (0 used) | 3 Minor (1 used: Mechanical Regeneration) |
   | Auxiliary Armor, Stage 3 | 3 (0 used) | 3 Minor (2 used: Force Field, Mechanical Regeneration) |
   | Shohan Stealth Armor | 3 (0 used) | 3 Moderate (3 used: Force Field, Force Field Regeneration, Mechanical Regeneration; its Weapon Link carries a self-powered Shohan weapon) |
   | Breaching Pod | 3 Major (1 used), 3 Moderate (1 used) | 3 Major (1 used), 3 Moderate (2 used: Force Field, Force Field Regeneration) |
   | Prometheus | 9 Major (4 used) | 9 Major (6 used) |
   | Marauder (Force Field Revision) | 6 Major (4 used) | 6 Major (5 used) |
   | Shohan Shieldbearer | 6 (3 used) | 6 Major (5 used: restoration beam, 2 flare cannons, Force Field, Force Field Regeneration) |
   | Shohan Lancer | 3 (2 used) | 6 Major (Hyperspace Tap 3 + Jump Drive 3; 3 used) |
   | Gunboat | no power supply printed | needs one: 2 guns, Force Field and Force Field Regeneration = 4 Moderate slots |

2. ~~**Explosive charges.**~~ **Decided 2026-10-05:** contact charges pay full price (no Melee half price); charges, grenades and the Agni rounds are sold per 10, so the target column shows printed CR − 1. Grav Grenade is Tiny. In the errata.
7. **TSA fixes approved 2026-10-05:** Peltast Body 25; Field Kit Personal Supplies Moderate ×3; TK-Tac APC troop deck = 2 Major Hangars, Life Support Moderate ×2; Wyvern and Syren Bleed is the free Plasma Bleed (Major 3); Srivatsa Moderate Coil rank 1 (3 slots, as printed) and [Shohan Killer 2]. TSA complete.
8. **Shohan fixes approved 2026-10-05:** Pyre is its own Attack implementation (Battlefield only, +70: Counter: Armor, Force Field Overload, Aggravated Assault; carries [Time Heals No Wounds 3]); Pyre Lance 175 BP, CR 5. Stun Field: [Seeing Stars 3] Free, Area Medium. Stealth Armor: Moderate Weapon Link, [Silent Running 3]. Fold Bomb: Area Small, lingering armor reduction free (p185 errata). All four costed Shohan items match. Power fixes: Flare Drones (Assault, Sniper) and Gunboat gain a Moderate Hyperspace Tap; Lancer Armor 2 is Space, power 6 Major (3 used).
9. **Artemis approved 2026-10-06** (from PJ's build notes): drives as printed; + Manufacture (Major), Power 6 ranks, Maneuver 4 (Major). 3538 BP, CR 14 / CC 105 (printed 15 was old rounding). In the errata.
10. **Missiles approved 2026-10-06:** Space missiles carry a chassis (Moderate Reaction Drive + Major Coil, no Charge/Fuel). Hyper Missile: Medium, Restricted Technology Moderate, 285 BP, CR 4 (5 per 10, as printed). Grav Missile: Medium, 7x, 265 BP, CR 4 (5 per 10; printed 3). In the errata.
11. **Approved 2026-10-08:** Gravitic attacks = Attack cost + 5 ([Flattened]); Grav Blades rule (Major, 75 BP); Redemption Aegis Option 2 Magazine 6. Phantom Mk. 3 waiting on PJ's build sheet. Lamellar Matrix rule approved (spec §5.13, errata).
3. **New attributes from Chapter 5** (need a cost rule in the spec):
   - **Gravitic attack** (Grav Pulser: 10 / 20 / 40 base by scale; Area required; Slave power). Priced here like an Energy attack.
   - **Grav Blades** (Major only, 75 BP, replaces the Gravitic Drive; can buy multipliers).
   - **Pyre (Exotic) attack** (Minor 80 / Moderate 90 / Major 110; includes Counter: Armor, Aggravated Assault, Force Field Overload, [Time Heals No Wounds 3]). Priced as the flat cost.
   - **Lamellar Matrix** Force Field implementation (same costs; bypassable by aimed shots below 25%).
   - **Psi-Resonant Materials** (75 / 150 / 300; must buy Area and Major Bleed; 2 Power Slots). No item uses it.
   - **Anomalous** Power Supply (Auxiliary Armor), **Arboreal** Drive (Resistance mechs), **Hyperspace Portal** (priced here as a Major Hangar, like the Deployment Pod's teleport platform), **Force Field Link** (priced as a Major Link), **Counter: Recording**.
4. **Missiles sold per 10.** Basic, Plasma and Scrammer STA/S work as Small items with group pricing. The Space-scale Grav and Hyper Missiles have no printed size and don't fit any size cleanly.
5. **Power-line typos to confirm:** Srivatsa (Moderate Coil 2 gives 6 slots; prints 3), TK-Tac HK (prints 9 Moderate; Coil 1 gives 3), Wyvern (prints 2 Major; Fusion 1 gives 3), Troop Carrier Module (prints 3 Major from a Moderate Fusion), Shohan Stealth Armor (prints 0 used with a Force Field), Marauder FF revision (CR 7 but CC 21; CR 7 is CC 28).
6. **Plasma Bleed at a different magnitude.** TK-Tac HK, Wyvern, Syren, Vigilance and the Troop Carrier print Minor Bleeds on plasma guns whose free Bleed is Moderate or Major. Paid in full here (per the 09-27 ruling); the HK and Syren need it to match, the Wyvern goes over with it (it matches at 690 BP without).
